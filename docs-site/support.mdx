## Channels by tier

| Tier | Primary channel | First-response SLA (Asia/Kuala Lumpur business hours) |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | [Discord — `#help`](https://discord.gg/wakijo) | Best-effort, community-supported |
| **Team** | `support@wakijo.dev` | **2 business days** |
| **Agency** | `support@wakijo.dev` + private Discord channel | **1 business day** + 1 hr onboarding call |
| **Enterprise** | Slack Connect channel + named CSM | **Same business day**, named engineer for incidents |

A "business day" is Mon–Fri, 09:00–18:00 Asia/Kuala Lumpur, excluding
Malaysian public holidays.

  <Accordion title="Best-effort" icon="circle-half-stroke">

      roadmap.

    - Onboarding to other tools in your stack.

</AccordionGroup>

## How to file a great ticket

A well-written ticket gets resolved 5× faster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Version and commit hash">
    `git rev-parse HEAD` and the top of your local `CHANGELOG.md`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Environment">
    Node version (`node --version`), pnpm version, OS, dev or production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What you did">
    Exact commands or HTTP requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What you expected — and what happened">
    Logs, stack traces, full error envelope from the API:
    ```json
    { "success": false, "statusCode": 422, "error": "VALIDATION_ERROR", ... }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Minimal repro">
    A failing test, a curl command, or a 5-line snippet beats a 200-line
    dump.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  For security-sensitive reports, follow the [SECURITY.md disclosure process](https://github.com/wakijo/wa-kijo/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
  instead of the regular support channel. Do not paste secrets, tokens,
  or PII into a shared support thread.
</Warning>

## Severity matrix

| Severity | Description | Time-to-fix (Agency / Enterprise) |
|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Critical** | Production down or data at risk. No workaround. | Same business day, hotfix |
| **S2 — High** | Major feature broken; painful workaround. | 5 business days |
| **S3 — Medium** | Bug with a clear workaround. | Next minor release |
| **S4 — Low** | Cosmetic / docs / quality of life. | Best-effort |

## Office hours

Open office hours **every other Thursday, 14:00–15:00 Asia/Kuala Lumpur**
on the customer Discord voice channel. Anyone on Team tier or above can
join with no agenda.

## Refunds and seat changes

Email `billing@wakijo.dev` for invoices, seat counts, tier changes, or
refunds. Refund window: **14 days from purchase**, no questions asked,
provided the source has not been pushed to a public repository or
distributed externally.
