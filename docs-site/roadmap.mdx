## Considering (no commitment)

These are the most-asked items that we're evaluating. Vote with your
support tickets.

- **Helm chart** for Kubernetes deployments.
- **Inngest / Trigger.dev** as an alternative job system behind a
  common interface.
- **GraphQL adapter** generated from the Zod DTOs.
- **Public marketplace** for community-contributed wa'kijo modules.
- **A11y audit + WCAG 2.2 AA conformance** for the shipped frontend.
- **`@wa-kijo/admin` reference admin app** as a fourth `apps/*` example.

## What we won't build

These come up but are out of scope on purpose:

- A drag-and-drop UI builder, low-code editor, or visual schema designer.
- A CMS or marketing-site CMS.
- Support for non-Postgres databases.
- Support for Express, Hono, Drizzle, Mongoose, or Auth.js.
- A managed hosting service — buyers own their deployments.
- A free tier — wa'kijo is a commercial product.

For the full rationale see the [PRD § 7 — Out of scope](https://github.com/wakijo/wa-kijo/blob/main/docs/prd.md#7-out-of-scope-often-requested-but-deliberate).

## Influencing the roadmap

Customer needs drive prioritisation. To make a request count:

- **Solo / Team** — file a support ticket or post in the customer
  Discord. Many customers asking for the same thing moves it up.
- **Agency / Enterprise** — bring it to your office hours or 1:1 with
  the CSM. Enterprise tier explicitly includes roadmap influence as
  part of the contract.

We publish quarterly retrospectives in the customer Discord
`#announcements` channel — what we shipped, what we deferred, and
what we learned.
