This is the public roadmap. **Order is by dependency**, not by calendar date — the wider the range on the v1.0 estimate (15–22 months), the more uncertainty we're being honest about.

Shipped work is in the [Changelog](/changelog). Current tag: **v0.6.1** (2026-05-17).

---

## Scope

wa'kijo is the enterprise foundation under a single business module (wa'lawe, shipped as its own OSS repo). Apache 2.0. Multi-tenant auth, RBAC, billing (Stripe), domain modules, audit log, frontend shell — plus the enterprise hardening, compliance, commerce, and engine layers being built over the next ~15–22 months.

> ⚠ **Reversal note.** Before 2026-05-17, the roadmap described a "platform with pluggable modules" direction (Module Registry, Customization Layer, kernel governance). That direction is **reversed** for v1.0. See [ADR-0011](https://github.com/benharundev/wa-kijo/blob/main/docs/decisions/0011-reverse-platform-pivot.md) for the rationale.

## Considering (no commitment)

- **Helm chart** for Kubernetes deployments.
- **Inngest / Trigger.dev** as an alternative job system behind a common interface.
- **GraphQL adapter** generated from the Zod DTOs.
- **A11y audit + WCAG 2.2 AA conformance** for the shipped frontend.
- **`@wa-kijo/admin` reference admin app** as a fourth `apps/*` example.
- **Data residency** — region-pinned tenants for EU customers.

## What we won't build

Out of scope on purpose:

- A drag-and-drop UI builder, low-code editor, or visual schema designer.
- A CMS or marketing-site CMS.
- Support for non-Postgres databases (Postgres 16+ only).
- Support for Express, Hono, Drizzle, Mongoose, or Auth.js.
- A managed hosting service — buyers own their deployments.
- Module Registry / Customization Layer / pluggable-module platform mechanics. See [ADR-0011](https://github.com/benharundev/wa-kijo/blob/main/docs/decisions/0011-reverse-platform-pivot.md) for why this was reversed.

Full rationale in [PRD § 7 — Out of scope](https://github.com/benharundev/wa-kijo/blob/main/docs/prd.md#7-out-of-scope-often-requested-but-deliberate).
